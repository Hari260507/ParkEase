QUICK GUIDE

Understand Slot Colours

Check the colour before selecting a parking slot.

Green — Available

The slot is free and available for a new booking.

Red — Occupied

The slot already has an active booking.

Yellow — Ending Soon

The current booking is scheduled to end within 10 minutes.
